Who are we?

We are Canera Building Technologies, a specialized civil engineering firm dedicated to solving complex structural challenges through innovative materials and expert craftsmanship. With a deep understanding of building science and deterioration mechanisms, we deliver performance-driven solutions that restore, protect, and extend the life of structures.

From leak-proofing and surface treatments to foundation strengthening and durability upgrades, we provide tailored, chemical-based interventions rooted in engineering principles and real-world insights.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Science-backed, material-first approach
  • Specialized in retrofitting and protection
  • Deep on-site problem diagnosis
  • Proven experience across industries
  • Client-first process with transparent execution

Basement & Foundation
